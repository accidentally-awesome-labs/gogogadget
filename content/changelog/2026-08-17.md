### Added

- **File storage** behind a `Store` seam — R2 in production, local disk in
  development, with org-scoped keys and presigned downloads.
- **Outbound webhooks** — endpoint management, standard-webhooks signatures,
  queued delivery with retries and dead-lettering, replay, and an SSRF guard
  that is checked twice (at validation and at dial time).
- **In-app notifications** — per-user rows, a sidebar bell with an unread
  badge, and a server-sent event stream.
- **Recurring schedules** — a `schedules` table claimed by the worker's
  scheduler pass, with missed ticks skipped by design rather than replayed.
- **Usage metering** — fire-and-forget recording, flushed to the billing
  provider by a job.
- **AI** — an OpenAI-compatible `Completer` seam, metered and plan-limited.
- **Feature flags** — DB-backed, 30s cache, deterministic FNV bucketing, and
  per-org overrides.
- **Impersonation** — an admin "view as" session with an opaque cookie that
  never bypasses Clerk.
- **Project search** — Postgres full-text with an ILIKE fallback so partial
  tokens still match.
- **Internationalisation** — locale detection middleware and English/Spanish
  catalogs.

### Fixed

- Pinned Tailwind's content sources: auto-detection was scanning gitignored
  test output and leaking phantom utilities into the committed stylesheet.
- Bumped Go to 1.26.6 for six stdlib advisories reachable from new code paths.
