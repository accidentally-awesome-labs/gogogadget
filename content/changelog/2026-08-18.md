---
title: Admin viewers, self-serve, and a testing foundation
date: 2026-08-18
summary: The admin surface grew four viewers, users got GDPR self-serve and notification preferences, and the test suite grew a floor.
---

### Added

- **Admin audit-log viewer** at `/admin/audit` and a **jobs viewer** at
  `/admin/jobs`, including dead-letter requeue.
- **Platform announcements** — admin CRUD and a dismissible app-shell banner,
  with at most one active at a time enforced by a partial unique index.
- **Notification preferences** at `/app/settings/notifications` — per-kind,
  default-on, and a preferences lookup that fails open rather than dropping a
  notification.
- **GDPR self-serve** — a one-click JSON data export and email-confirmed
  account deletion with org-cascade safety checks.
- **A dedicated 403 page** and an env-gated maintenance mode that sheds
  traffic with a 503 (JSON under `/api/`) while probes stay green.
- **Mobile navigation** — the authed shell had a sidebar that was
  `hidden md:flex`, so phone users could not navigate at all.

### Changed

- **Testing foundation** — contract suites for every seam, coverage for
  previously untested packages and routes, fuzz tests at the two trust
  boundaries, and CI hardened with `-race`, a fuzz pass, a smoke job, and a
  docker build.
- Published `/docs/roadmap`: an evidence-linked gap report of what a
  production SaaS needs, tiered, including what is deliberately delegated.
